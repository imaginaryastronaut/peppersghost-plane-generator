This generates a printable template for cutting out a reflective plane to go inside a cylindrical specimen jar at an angle of 45 degrees, creating a Pepper's Ghost effect if the jar is placed on a screen of some sort.
To achieve a Pepper's Ghost effect, the reflective plane, eg acrylic or acetate sheet, needs to be at an angle of 45 degrees inside the jar, angled towards the viewer. So the back end of the plane is lower than the front end of the plane
Depending on the height of the jar, you can choose where you'd like your reflective plane to sit in the jar (you might need to fix the plane to the sides of the jar with silicon sticky dots or tape)
To measure where the sides of your jar start to curve away, hold a ruler flush against the side of the upright jar and measure where the glass starts to curve away from the ruler.
